Jan Dítě is a small waiter with big plans: to become a millionaire and to be seen. The book follows
him through hotels, the war and what came after, told in long, breathless sentences.

<!-- more -->

Hrabal wrote it in 1971, but for years it could only be passed around as a typescript. The title
comes from a line the head waiter says whenever someone asks how he knows everything:
"I served the King of England."
