A short novel from 1965 about Miloš, a young apprentice at a sleepy railway station during the war.
Very little happens at the station, and then far too much.

<!-- more -->

Jiří Menzel turned it into a film a year later, and the film won an Oscar. It was the first of
the Hrabal books he filmed, over forty years:

1. Ostře sledované vlaky
2. Skřivánci na niti
3. Postřižiny
4. Slavnosti sněženek
5. Obsluhoval jsem anglického krále

> [!TIP]
> Read the book first. It's short, and the film makes more sense afterwards.
