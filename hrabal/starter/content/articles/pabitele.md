A *pábitel* is someone who talks and talks, tells stories that grow with every telling, and sees the
world a little brighter than it is. Hrabal made the word up and gave it to a book of stories in 1964.

<!-- more -->

Most of his characters are pábitelé, and Uncle Pepin from [Postřižiny](@/articles/postriziny.md) is the
greatest of them.
